## Capabilities

- FBE/metadata `/data` decryption and User 0 synthetic-password decryption
- `/data/media/0`, MTP, and ADB access
- Qualcomm A/B boot HAL for slot switching
- QCA6750 WiFi driver/firmware/supplicant stack
- OPlus vibrator, health/charger, boot, KeyMint, Gatekeeper, and QSEE services
- Touch payload and Prado firmware
- 60 fps TWRP GUI and local input-latency patch
- `7za`, `zstd`, and full partition tool set

## Verification

The image was flashed to both recovery slots. ADB appeared after 54 seconds and
remained online. After PIN entry:

```text
crypto.ready=1
twrp.user.0.decrypt=1
/data mounted RW as F2FS
/data/media/0 visible
```

Known non-blocking issue: `init.svc.touch-aidl` may report `restarting` while
touch remains functional.

## Safety

- Do not wipe or format `/data`.
- Wait at least 90 seconds before declaring a recovery boot failed.
- Do not re-enable `TW_USE_NEW_MINADBD`; it caused a cold boot without USB/ADB.
- Do not restore the failed v17 `keystore2.rc` interface override.
- Keep the upstream rtyutechstudio clean image locally as rollback.
